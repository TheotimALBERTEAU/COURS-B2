## PAST SIMPLE OR PRESENT PERFECT

1. I **visited** London when I was eleven years old.
2. Pam **has already seen** this adventure film today.
3. My son **drew** a very funny pony yesterday.
4. Liz **has not done** her homework yet.
5. My uncle **was born** in a small village in 1986.
6. We **have lost** the football match in our yard recently.
7. **Have** you **ever visited** any European countries?
8. Tom **kept** two hamsters at home last year.
9. My parents **have just arrived** from a trip to London.
10. Why **did** you **not answer** my phone call yesterday?
11. How much money **have** you **already spent** on clothes today?
12. The boys **spent** much time at the lion’s cage yesterday.
13. My brother **has just shown** us his new car. It’s very expensive.
14. Ben **met** his friend at the railway station last Monday.
15. Mike **has broken** his left arm this morning.
16. **Did** you **go** to the concert of classical music yesterday?
17. Helen **has just won** the lottery in the shoe shop.
18. Andrew **did not see** Tim at school yesterday.
19. Where **did** you **live** when you were eleven years old?
20. They **have lived** in New York for six months.
21. I **did not go** to the swimming pool last week.
22. **Have** you **ever eaten** Mexican food in this restaurant?
23. Mona **wore** a beautiful blue dress at the party yesterday.
24. That’s the best play I **have ever seen** this month.
25. Bill **sold** his old car three days ago.
26. Philip **has just flown** to Los Angeles for his holidays.
27. I **did not drive** my car yesterday because I was very tired.
28. Mary **has just found** an interesting report about Egypt.
29. What present **did** you **choose** for your sister’s birthday?
30. Nick **worked** as a waiter in the restaurant last year.
31. **Did** your brother **start** school last year or two years ago?
32. Where **did** you **stay** in Paris last August?
33. **Has** your brother **ever been** to Australia?
34. **Did** you **help** your mother in the kitchen yesterday?
35. How many books **have** you **already bought** for school?
36. My basketball team **did not win** the game yesterday.
