# Australia

**Have** you ever **been** to Australia? I **went** there about 5 years ago. First, I **stopped** in Sydney. I **saw** the Opera House while I **was** there, but I **have not walked** across the Harbour Bridge yet. I **have** already **been** to Brisbane, but I still **have not been** to Cairns. I **heard** from a friend last week that Cairns is beautiful. **Have** you **heard** of Ayers Rock? I **have not gone** there yet because it’s pretty far from Sydney.

When I **was** in Australia last time, I **traveled** up the Gold Coast. I **learned** to surf in Surfer’s paradise. It **was** fun! But I **have** never **tried** parasailing.

### For or Since

- Have you lived here **for** many years?
    
- He has thought about going to Africa **since** July 2012.
    
- My parents have been in California **for** two months.
    
- Marco has dreamed about going to Brazil **since** he was a child.
    
- She hasn’t been to Mexico **since** she was six years old.
    
- Julia has wanted to travel to China **since** she saw a TV show about it.
    
- I travelled all over the world **for** the past eight months.
    
- We have camped all over this island **since** the beginning of the year.

### Fill in the blanks with _already_, _still_, or _yet_

**A:** I’m trying to decide where to go on my next vacation. Have you been to Venice? I **still** haven’t been to Italy. **B:** No, but I’ve **already** been to Europe, and I want to go to another continent. **A:** What about Korea? I’ve **already** been to Seoul, and I loved it. **B:** Korea sounds great, but I wanted to do something more adventurous. **A:** Okay, I haven’t been to Nepal **yet**, but I’d love to go hiking there. How about Nepal? **B:** I’ve **already** been to Nepal. It was amazing! **A:** Hmmm. Well, how about going on a safari in Africa? **B:** Good idea! I haven’t been on a safari **yet**. **A:** Me neither. Let’s go together!
