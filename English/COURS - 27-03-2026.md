# COMPANIES – TENSES EXERCISES

### Exercise 1: Simple Present vs. Present Continuous

1. Our company **offers** (offer) various financial consulting services.
2. Right now, the CEO **is discussing** (discuss) the annual report with the board of directors.
3. John usually **works** (work) from home, but today he **is attending** (attend) a conference in New York.
4. The marketing team **is launching** (launch) a new campaign this week.
5. Clients always **expect** (expect) high-quality service from our firm.
6. We **are not accepting** (not/accept) new project proposals at the moment.
7. The manager **schedules** (schedule) meetings every Monday morning.
8. Look! The employees **are preparing** (prepare) for the annual product presentation.
9. Our IT department **is constantly improving** (constantly/improve) the security system.
10. The company **is implementing** (implement) sustainable business practices to reduce costs and environmental impact.

### Exercise 2: Preterit vs. Present Perfect Simple

4. The HR department **hired** (hire) three new employees last month.
5. **Have you ever negotiated** (you / ever / negotiate) a contract with an international client?
6. She **attended** (attend) a leadership seminar two weeks ago.
7. They **have never missed** (never / miss) a deadline since they started working here.
8. We **have not received** (not / receive) feedback on the project yet.
9. When **did you first meet** (you / first / meet) your current manager?
10. The company **has won** (win) several awards for innovation over the past decade.
