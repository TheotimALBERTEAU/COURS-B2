## BUSINESS TRAVEL
Oscar travels **for** work often. He goes **to** Zurich at least once a month. Usually he spends a few days **in** Zurich. 

Oscar lives **in** Barcelona. He likes to travel **to** Zurich early **in** the morning **on** Mondays. He packs his suitcase carefully **in** the evening **before** his travel. Oscar gets up **at** 5.30 am, drinks a glass of water, dresses and leaves the house immediately. He goes **to** the airport **by** taxi. 

Oscar arrives **at** the airport **at** 6 am. He usually prints his boarding pass **at** the office in advance the day before and goes straight **to** the security check counter. He only has one small carry-on suitcase – he does not have to check it **in**. Security officer asks Oscar to take **out** his computer and his plastic bag with small personal hygiene products. He also asks Oscar to take **off** his shoes and his belt. 

Security check is over. Oscar packs his computer back **in** the suitcase and goes **to** the information screen to check the status of his flight. It is still too early **for** the flight, therefore, there is no information about it **on** the information screen. Oscar goes **to** the café to have a sandwich and a cup of coffee. He finds a newspaper **on** the table and reads it. 

It is 6.30. Oscar finishes his coffee and goes **to** the information screen. The information screen shows his flight status “Go to the gate”. The gate number is 45. Oscar finds his gate and sits **down** to wait until boarding time. **At** 6.45 the airport staff announces that the flight to Zurich No LX 156 is now boarding. Oscar sits **in** the lounge and waits for the majority of passengers to board the plane. He presents his boarding pass **to** the airport staff and boards the plane **at** the last minute. The flight attendant shows him his seat **on** the plane and offers him a newspaper. 

Flight attendants give safety instructions. Oscar listens carefully and tightens his safety belt. The plane leaves Barcelona **on** time - **at** 7.00am. Oscar is sleepy and takes another cup of coffee in the plane with a croissant. He reads La Vanguardia **about** the Spanish economic crisis **during** the flight. Oscar looks **out** the window to see the snowy Alps. 

Time passes quickly and the plane lands **in** Zurich **on** time - **at** 8.30am. Oscar takes his suitcase, thanks the flight attendants and rushes **to** work. He takes a train to go **to** his office in Zurich. The train arrives quickly and takes only 15 minutes to go **to** the city center. Oscar gets off **at** the ”Hauptbanhoff” station and walks another 5 minutes. He is **at** his Zurich office **at** 9am. He calls his wife to tell that everything is ok, says hello to his colleagues and starts his computer.
